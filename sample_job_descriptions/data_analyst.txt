Job Title: Senior Data Analyst & BI Specialist
Department: Business Intelligence & Data Analytics
Location: New York, NY / Hybrid

About the Role:
We are looking for a Senior Data Analyst to empower business decision-making through actionable insights, interactive dashboards, and advanced data modeling.

Key Responsibilities:
• Extract, transform, and analyze complex datasets from enterprise data warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery).
• Design, build, and maintain executive BI dashboards in Tableau and Power BI.
• Write optimized SQL queries and conduct exploratory data analysis using Python, Pandas, and NumPy.
• Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to define KPIs and deliver data-driven recommendations.
• Build automated ETL data pipelines and validate data integrity with Excel modeling.

Required Qualifications & Skills:
• Bachelor's or Master's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or Business Analytics.
• 3+ years of experience in data analysis, BI reporting, and business intelligence.
• Advanced SQL skills and proficiency in Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib/Seaborn).
• Proven track record with Tableau and Power BI dashboard creation.
• Experience with cloud data warehouses such as Snowflake or BigQuery, and ETL processes.
• Exceptional analytical, problem solving, and presentation skills.
